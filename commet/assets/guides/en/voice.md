A **voice room** is a room you can drop into and talk, like a voice channel on Discord. It has a speaker icon in the room list.

[[picture: voice]]

## Joining and leaving

Click a voice room to join it. **Nobody gets rung**: people come and go whenever they like, and the room list shows who's in it.

Once you're in, the buttons at the bottom mute your microphone, turn on your camera, share your screen, or hang up.

## Your microphone and speakers

Pick your microphone, speakers and camera in **Settings › Voice and Video**.

## Calls in other rooms

Any room can have a call, not only voice rooms. Vommet works with calls started in Element Call and Element X, and people using those apps can join from there.

See also: [Rooms and joining](guide:rooms)
