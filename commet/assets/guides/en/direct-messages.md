A **direct message** (DM) is a private chat with one person or a small group. Your DMs are on **Home**, the house icon at the top of the space icons.

[[picture: home]]

## Start one

Start a new chat from Home and search for the person's name or Matrix address (it looks like **@name:example.org**). They get an invite and can accept or ignore it.

## Is it really them?

Direct messages are encrypted. If Vommet can't confirm someone's identity, it says so at the top of the chat. Verifying each other removes the doubt. See [Encryption and your devices](guide:encryption).

## Favorites

Right-click a chat (or press and hold it) and make it a favorite to keep it at the top of Home.
