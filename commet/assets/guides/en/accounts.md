Vommet is an app for **Matrix**, an open chat network. There's no single Matrix company: anyone can run a Matrix server, called a **homeserver**, and they all talk to each other.

[[picture: accounts]]

## Your address

Your account lives on one homeserver, and your address includes its name, like **@mossfox:example.org**. People on any other homeserver can still message you, join your rooms and call you, the same way email works between providers.

## Which homeserver?

Any homeserver works with Vommet. Some are open to everyone, some need an invite. When you create an account, Vommet suggests a few. You can sign in to more than one account in **Settings › Manage Accounts**.
