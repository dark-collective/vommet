A **space** is a group of rooms, like a server on Discord. Your spaces are the icons down the left side. Click one to see its rooms.

[[picture: spaces]]

## How spaces differ from Discord servers

| On Discord | On Vommet |
| --- | --- |
| Joining a server puts you in every channel. | Joining a space doesn't join its rooms. You pick the ones you want. |
| Servers live on Discord's computers. | Rooms can live on any Matrix server, and a space can mix rooms from many servers. |
| A server can't contain another server. | A space can hold other spaces (subspaces), like folders. |

## Make your own

Click the **+** below your space icons (**Add Space**) and create a new space. Give it a name and a picture, then add rooms to it. To bring people in, open the space's home (click its name) and use **Invite**.

## Join one

The same **+** joins a space from an address or link someone sent you. The explore button below it finds public spaces and rooms.
