A **room** is one conversation, like a channel on Discord. Some rooms are for text, some for voice.

## Why a new space can look empty

Joining a space **doesn't join its rooms**. The room list only shows rooms you're in, so a space you just joined can look nearly empty.

To see the rest, right-click the space's icon (or press and hold it) and pick **Browse rooms**. Or click the space's name at the top of the room list: every room you haven't joined yet is under **Available rooms**.

## Things you can do with a room

Right-click a room in the list (or press and hold it on a phone) to mark it as read, make it a favorite, or leave it. Favorites stay at the top of Home.

A room can belong to more than one space, and it can live on a different server from the space it's in. Either way it works the same.
