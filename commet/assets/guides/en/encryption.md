Encrypted chats can only be read by the people in them, on their own devices. Not even the server can read them. Direct messages are encrypted, and so are most private rooms.

## What the icons after a room's name mean

[[picture: shield]]

No icon is the normal case: the room is encrypted and nothing needs your attention. An icon means something is different:

- **Grey**: this room isn't encrypted. That's normal for big public rooms.
- **Green**: you've verified the person in this direct message.
- **Amber**: this direct message isn't encrypted, often because it's a bot or a bridge.
- **Red**: the person's identity changed. If they didn't tell you they reset it, someone could be pretending to be them.

## Your recovery key

Your messages are locked with keys stored on your devices. The **recovery key** you saved when you set up secure messaging unlocks them on a new device. Keep it somewhere safe, like a password manager. Without it, a new device can't read your old messages.

## Verifying

Verifying confirms that a person, or one of your own devices, is really who it says. Open someone's profile and pick **Verify**, then compare the emoji with them. Your own devices are in **Settings › Security**.
